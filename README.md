#  FinSight — Banking Executive Intelligence Dashboard

A premium fintech-style executive intelligence dashboard built using **Power BI, Python, SQL, DAX, and Power Query** to analyze banking customer behavior, conversion performance, and campaign effectiveness.

---

##  Project Overview

FinSight is a modern business intelligence project designed to simulate a real-world executive analytics dashboard used in banking and fintech organizations.

The dashboard provides decision-makers with actionable insights into:

- Customer conversion performance
- Campaign effectiveness
- Customer segmentation
- Seasonal trends
- Contact channel performance
- Loan-based customer behavior

---

##  Business Problem

Banks invest heavily in marketing campaigns to convert customers into term deposit subscribers.

However, business leaders need visibility into:

- Which customer groups convert best?
- Which outreach channel performs the strongest?
- How conversion changes across months
- Whether loan customers present stronger opportunities
- Overall campaign effectiveness

FinSight addresses these challenges through data analytics and executive reporting.

##  Key Insights

### 1. Strong Campaign Performance
- **11K+ customers analyzed**
- **5K+ successful conversions**
- **47% conversion rate**

This indicates strong overall campaign effectiveness.

---

### 2. High-Value Customer Segments
Management professionals show the highest conversion performance, followed by technicians and blue-collar customers.

---

### 3. Best Performing Outreach Channel
Cellular contact significantly outperforms traditional phone outreach.

This suggests mobile-first customer engagement strategies are more effective.

---

### 4. Seasonal Conversion Trends
Conversions peak in high-performing campaign months and drop during lower engagement periods.

This can help optimize campaign timing.

---

### 5. Cross-Selling Opportunity
A large share of converted customers already hold housing loans.

This highlights opportunities for cross-selling financial products.
